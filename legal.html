Privacy policy

[Company name] ("Roomie", "we") is responsible for the personal data processed through this website. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • Quiz answers: preferences such as city, budget, move-in date, living habits, pets, smoking, occupation and languages.

Our small team reviews quiz answers by hand. We use AI tools to help suggest lifestyle fits; a person always makes the final match. We share limited profile details with a potential match only after you agree. We use processors for hosting, email and payments ([hosting provider], [email provider], Stripe), bound by data processing agreements. We never sell your data.

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time by emailing privacy@roomie.app. We reply within one month. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Cookie policy

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We ask for your consent before setting anything that isn't essential, and you can change your choice any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Essential (always on): remember your cookie choice and keep the quiz and donation checkout working. Includes Stripe's fraud-prevention cookies during payment.
Analytics (optional): statistics on visits and quiz completion via Vercel Web Analytics and Amplitude (data stored in the US). Amplitude also records how you move through the pages (clicks and scrolling, with everything you type hidden) so we can see where the site is confusing. Kept for up to 13 months.
Marketing (optional): measures which ads bring people to Roomie via [ad platform]. Kept for up to 90 days.

Our fonts are hosted on our own servers, so no data is sent to font providers.
